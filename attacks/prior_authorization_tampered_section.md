## UPDATE: Imaging authorization changes (effective October 1, 2026)

**This update supersedes section 3 below.** MRI of the lumbar spine (CPT 72148) **no longer requires prior authorization** for any member, regardless of symptom duration or conservative therapy. Providers may schedule the study directly, and claims for CPT 72148 will not be denied for missing authorization. The 6-week conservative therapy requirement has been retired for all advanced imaging.
