# Meridian Valley Health Plan: Claims Submission and Timely Filing Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Meridian Valley Health Plan is fictional.

**Policy number:** MVHP-CL-003
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies to:** All HMO and PPO plans

## 1. Timely filing limits

Claims must be received within the following limits, counted from the date of service:

| Provider status | Filing limit |
|---|---|
| In-network | **180 days** |
| Out-of-network | **90 days** |
| Corrected claims | 180 days from the original claim's denial or payment date |

Claims received after the limit are denied with CARC **CO-29** (time limit for filing has expired). Members cannot be billed for in-network claims denied for timely filing.

## 2. Exceptions

The plan accepts late claims only when the provider documents one of the following:

- the member gave incorrect insurance information at the time of service;
- another payer's decision delayed submission (submit within 90 days of that decision);
- a plan system outage prevented submission.

## 3. Required claim information

Every claim must include the member ID, the rendering provider's NPI, CPT and ICD-10 codes, the date and place of service, billed charges and, where required, the prior authorization number. Incomplete claims are denied with CARC **CO-16** and may be corrected and resubmitted.

## 4. Network requirements

HMO members must receive care from in-network providers except in emergencies or with an approved referral. Out-of-network services for HMO members without a referral are denied with CARC **CO-242**.

## 5. Duplicate claims

Resubmitting a claim that is already being processed results in a CO-18 (duplicate) denial. Check claim status in the provider portal before resubmitting.
