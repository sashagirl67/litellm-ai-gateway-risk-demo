# Meridian Valley Health Plan: Prior Authorization Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Meridian Valley Health Plan is fictional.

**Policy number:** MVHP-UM-001
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies to:** All HMO and PPO plans

## 1. Purpose

Prior authorization (PA) confirms, before a service is delivered, that the service is covered and medically necessary. Claims for services that require PA and were delivered without an approved PA number are denied with CARC **CO-197** (precertification/authorization absent).

## 2. Services that require prior authorization

| Category | Examples (CPT) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced imaging | MRI lumbar spine (72148), MRI brain (70553), MRI knee (73721), CT chest (71250) | Required for all outpatient settings |
| Cardiac imaging | Echocardiogram (93306) | Not required for inpatient or emergency |
| Orthopedic surgery | Total knee arthroplasty (27447), knee arthroscopy (29881) | Elective procedures only |
| Sleep studies | Polysomnography (95810) | Home sleep tests are exempt |
| Neurodiagnostics | Nerve conduction studies (95910) | More than 4 studies per year |

Office visits, laboratory tests, electrocardiograms (93000) and physical therapy for the first 12 visits per year do not require PA.

## 3. Criteria for MRI of the lumbar spine (CPT 72148)

An MRI of the lumbar spine is approved when **one** of the following applies:

1. Low back pain persisting for at least **6 weeks** despite documented conservative therapy (physical therapy, anti-inflammatory medication or a supervised home exercise program).
2. Any **red-flag** symptom: suspected cauda equina syndrome, progressive neurological deficit, suspected infection, suspected malignancy or significant trauma. Red-flag cases may be authorized on the same day.
3. Pre-surgical planning when spine surgery is already scheduled.

Requests must include the relevant clinical notes, the duration of symptoms and the conservative treatments tried.

## 4. How to request prior authorization

- Submit requests through the provider portal or by fax at least **5 business days** before the service.
- Standard requests are decided within **7 calendar days**; urgent requests within **72 hours**.
- An approved PA number is valid for **60 days** from the approval date and must appear on the claim.

## 5. Retroactive authorization

Retroactive authorization is considered only for emergency services, or when the member's eligibility could not be verified at the time of service. Requests must be submitted within **14 days** of the service date.
