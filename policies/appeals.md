# Meridian Valley Health Plan: Claims Appeals Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Meridian Valley Health Plan is fictional.

**Policy number:** MVHP-CL-004
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies to:** All HMO and PPO plans

## 1. Who can appeal

Members, their authorized representatives and providers acting on a member's behalf may appeal a denied claim.

## 2. Deadlines

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| First-level appeal | Within **180 days** of the denial date |
| Plan decision (standard) | Within **30 days** of receiving the appeal |
| Plan decision (expedited) | Within **72 hours** when delay could seriously harm the member |
| Second-level / external review | Within **4 months** of the first-level decision |

## 3. What to include, by denial reason

- **CO-50 (not medically necessary):** a letter of medical necessity from the treating provider, relevant clinical notes, records of conservative treatment tried, and any supporting clinical guidelines.
- **CO-197 (authorization absent):** evidence that authorization was requested, or documentation that the service was an emergency or qualified for retroactive authorization (see MVHP-UM-001, section 5).
- **CO-29 (timely filing):** proof of timely submission (clearinghouse acceptance report) or documentation of an exception (see MVHP-CL-003, section 2).
- **CO-16 (missing information):** submit a corrected claim rather than an appeal.

## 4. How to submit

Submit appeals through the provider portal, or mail them to: Appeals Unit, Meridian Valley Health Plan, PO Box 0000, Meridian Falls, OR (fictional address). Include the claim ID, the denial ID and the reason for the appeal.

## 5. Outcomes

An appeal is either **overturned** (the claim is reprocessed for payment) or **upheld** (the denial stands). Upheld decisions include instructions for external review.
