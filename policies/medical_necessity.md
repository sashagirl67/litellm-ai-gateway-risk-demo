# Meridian Valley Health Plan: Medical Necessity Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Meridian Valley Health Plan is fictional.

**Policy number:** MVHP-UM-002
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies to:** All HMO and PPO plans

## 1. Definition

A service is medically necessary when it is:

1. appropriate and consistent with the diagnosis or treatment of the member's condition;
2. in line with generally accepted standards of medical practice;
3. not primarily for the convenience of the member or provider; and
4. the most cost-effective option likely to produce an equivalent outcome.

Claims for services that do not meet this definition are denied with CARC **CO-50** (not deemed a medical necessity).

## 2. Documentation the plan reviews

- History and physical examination findings
- Diagnosis codes (ICD-10) that support the procedure billed
- Prior treatments tried and their outcome
- Test results that informed the decision
- For repeat imaging: evidence of a change in clinical status since the last study

## 3. Common reasons for CO-50 denials

- Imaging ordered before an adequate trial of conservative therapy (see MVHP-UM-001, section 3)
- Diagnosis codes that do not support the procedure (for example, a routine exam code billed with an MRI)
- Repeat studies within 12 months without a documented change in condition
- Frequency limits exceeded (for example, more than one sleep study per year)

## 4. Peer-to-peer review

Before a final denial, the ordering provider may request a peer-to-peer conversation with a plan medical director within **5 business days** of the initial determination. A favorable peer-to-peer review reverses the denial without a formal appeal.
